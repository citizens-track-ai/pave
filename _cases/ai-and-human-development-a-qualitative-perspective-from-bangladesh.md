# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "This report presents the results of a\
  \ series of consultations conducted across Bangladesh to understand how different\
  \ people perceive and experience AI technologies. Through 32 Focus Group Discussions\
  \ (FGDs) involving 240 participants from five distinct socio-demographic groups,\
  \ and 20 in-depth interviews (IDI), we uncover nuanced insights into AI perceptions,\
  \ engagement, trust dynamics, and barriers to equitable adoption. We analyze how\
  \ participants conceptualize and represent AI, and how different groups actively\
  \ or passively engage with AI in their daily lives. Our findings highlight significant\
  \ disparities in awareness and literacy across groups; underline widespread concerns\
  \ regarding transparency and trust; and highlight the complex interplay between\
  \ technological advancement and socio-economic inequalities. \n"

# Why is this project important to include in the case book?
why-is-this-project-important-to-include-in-the: "The report uncovers several pressing\
  \ issues related to the rapid development and deployment of AI technologies in a\
  \ country where only half the population was reported to have access to the internet\
  \ in 2023. Notably, information integrity and the burden of responsibility for fact-checking\
  \ emerge as critical concerns, especially amid Bangladesh\u2019s sensitive political\
  \ climate since the 2024 July Uprising. AI-driven content recommendations are also\
  \ perceived as politically, culturally, or religiously biased by many participants,\
  \ causing frustration and distrust, even as younger users may otherwise value such\
  \ systems for their ability to deliver relevant educational and professional content.\
  \ AI\u2019s potential for surveillance and its opaque decision-making processes,\
  \ which can restrict user agency and autonomy on online platforms, further raise\
  \ concern. \n\nOur goal is to inform technology policy, design interventions, and\
  \ strategies for fostering equitable, trustworthy, and inclusive AI adoption in\
  \ Bangladesh and countries with comparable technological landscapes across Global\
  \ Majority countries. The work was presented virtually at the PAIRS26 Symposium,\
  \ and it extends initial research we conducted in the UNDP Accelerator Labs Network\
  \ with the Columbia University School of International and Public Affairs (SIPA)\
  \ in Morocco and Paraguay, which was presented at the PAIRS25 Symposium in Paris,\
  \ France. "

# Inclusion efforts
inclusion-efforts: 'We centered our discussions on specific, widely used applications
  (such as Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, bKash) familiar to the participants as accessible
  entry points. Grounding conversations in these widely recognized platforms and providing
  concrete, relatable examples facilitated inclusive and accessible discussions on
  AI which enabled participants, from digitally proficient content creators to individuals
  with minimal technological exposure, to meaningfully engage with the concept, share
  nuanced reflections, and articulate diverse perceptions around trust, agency, and
  the impact of AI on their lives.

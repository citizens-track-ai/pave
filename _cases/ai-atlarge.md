# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "Launched in 2026, AI AtLarge is exploring\
  \ the use of capacity-building workshops and structured listening to help everyday\
  \ communities navigate and question the impact of artificial intelligence on their\
  \ daily lives. By focusing on practical oversight questions rather than technical\
  \ training, the project aims to empower workers, families, and students to participate\
  \ meaningfully in AI governance.\n\nThe team behind it explain: \n\n> \"AI will\
  \ affect everyone, yet the public discussion around it often remains distant from\
  \ the people whose work, education, families, public services, and daily lives are\
  \ being reshaped. AI AtLarge exists to help people understand, use, question, navigate,\
  \ and shape artificial intelligence through accessible resources, public dialogue,\
  \ civic engagement, and carefully selected community-led activity.\n> \n> Our purpose\
  \ is not simply to teach people how AI works. It is to build the knowledge and confidence\
  \ people need to recognize where AI affects them, ask informed questions, and participate\
  \ meaningfully in decisions that shape their lives.\"\n\nAI AtLarge is currently\
  \ operating as a focused public-interest platform. Its initial work includes clear\
  \ public education, practical tools, structured public listening, short public notes\
  \ that translate institutional AI debates into understandable questions, and selective\
  \ engagement in forums concerning AI\u2019s societal effects. It may co-host events\
  \ where a credible partner provides a clear audience, practical capacity, and public\
  \ benefit.\n\nGatherings, local Hubs, and Chapters remain possible future delivery\
  \ mechanisms. AI AtLarge does not yet claim to represent AI users or to operate\
  \ a broad network. It intends to earn legitimacy through sustained listening, useful\
  \ public resources, and credible engagement with people living with AI\u2019s effects.\n\
  \n**Primary aims**\n- Advance public understanding of AI in everyday life.\n- Strengthen\
  \ people\u2019s confidence and human agency in an AI-shaped society.\n- Make AI\
  \ governance questions understandable and relevant beyond expert communities.\n\
  - Listen to real public questions, experiences, concerns, and aspirations.\n- Support\
  \ more meaningful public participation in decisions about AI.\n\n**Who the initiative\
  \ serves**\nPeople living with the effects of AI, particularly workers, families,\
  \ students, seniors, consumers, and communities not ordinarily included in the traditional\
  \ AI ecosystem.\n\n**Approach / methodology**\nAI AtLarge combines accessible public\
  \ resources, practical questions, structured listening, and selective civic engagement.\
  \ Its educational approach can draw on four questions from the _Human in the Oversight_\
  \ framework:\n\n1. Can people see where and how AI affects them?\n2. Can they question,\
  \ correct, refuse, appeal, or obtain meaningful human review?\n3. Who is accountable\
  \ when an AI-enabled system causes harm or unfairness?\n4. What evidence justifies\
  \ trust in its use?\n\nThis is intended as a practical public-interest framework,\
  \ not a technical training curriculum.\n\n**Current activities**\n- Accessible resources\
  \ on AI in everyday life.\n- An \u201CAI Ask Anything\u201D public-learning and\
  \ listening channel.\n- Selective participation in public hearings, forums, and\
  \ institutional discussions.\n- Short factual public notes translating AI policy\
  \ and governance developments into plain-language questions.\n- Occasional co-hosted\
  \ public events with credible partners.\n- Gradual development of an AI Public Experience\
  \ Observatory based on real questions and experiences.\n\n**Geographic scope**\n\
  Initially United States-based, with a public-interest perspective relevant to wider\
  \ international AI-governance discussions. Future activity may include international\
  \ partnerships where there is a credible public benefit and delivery capacity.\n\
  \n\n"

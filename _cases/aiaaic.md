# Why is this project important to include in the case book?
why-is-this-project-important-to-include-in-the: 'Although very imperfect in the way
  it involves the public (the project is open and voluntary but lacks mechanisms to
  enhance public engagement and actively reach out to affected communities), the AIAAIC
  has been one of the pioneers when it comes to registering AI systems harms, its
  database has been used to seed all other influential projects in that direction,
  and remains the only open and independent incident repository.

  It has been used and cited extensively in academic research, journalism and CSO
  advocacy.

  Its harms taxonomy (of which I am a co-author, so I might be biased) has also been
  influential and was one of the first published taxonomy to focus on harms and not
  on risks.


  In spite of its limits, it is a project of historical and thematic signifiance to
  the case book (and arguably, its limits are also interesting to evaluate: difficulty
  of maintaining an active community with a global scope, lack of resources despite
  relative influence...).'

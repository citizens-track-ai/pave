# Headline findings
headline-findings: 'No final report as project is ongoing, but the team identify the
  following areas which have often arisen in the group discussions that we have been
  a part of:

  - Popular fear around AI, not just from elderly participants, but also including
  the (very) young. Often linked to a perceived lack of regulation, control, and its
  impact on our society.

  - Distrust of being marketed/"forced" to use AI solutions.

  - Policies which are perceived to be lacking in environmental protection, such as
  the construction of data centres, as well as the impact of AI on our information
  (elections...).

  '

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "Caf\xE9 AI is a public movement which\
  \ aims to promote a shared, accessible, and democratic culture around artificial\
  \ intelligence and digital technologies. Our mission is to empower individuals to\
  \ make informed and conscious decisions about their individual and collective uses\
  \ of AI and digital technologies, whether they choose to adopt them, adapt them,\
  \ or limit their presence in their lives.\n\nLaunched in France in 2024 on the request\
  \ of President Macron by the French National Digital Council (Conseil national du\
  \ num\xE9rique), the movement is today by an independent state project, the Mission\
  \ Caf\xE9 AI (Mission Caf\xE9 IA), hosted by the Directorate General of Enterprises\
  \ (Direction g\xE9n\xE9rale des entreprises) of the Ministry of the Economy and\
  \ Finance (Minist\xE8re de l\u2019\xE9conomie et des finances).\n\nCaf\xE9 AI is\
  \ above all a space for discussion, where individuals can learn, experiment, and\
  \ debate as part of a shared digital culture. We work with our team of over 1000\
  \ volunteers, digital mediation professionals, and professional structures to launch\
  \ discussion spaces across France, and increasingly across Europe. Alongside this,\
  \ we propose a large number of activities, games, learning modules and editorial\
  \ resources enabling citizens to understand better digital technologies. We work\
  \ to ensure that citizens have the tools they need to make enlightened decisions\
  \ about their own - and collective - uses of AI and digital technologies. \n\nWe\
  \ are aiming to involve 2 million French citizens by the end of 2027.\n"

# Why is this project important to include in the case book?
why-is-this-project-important-to-include-in-the: "Following your email expressing\
  \ interest, we are delighted to share details of the Caf\xE9 AI project for potential\
  \ inclusion. We believe Caf\xE9 AI is unique and important as it works to ensure\
  \ that citizens' voices are included in decisions and policies about AI and digital\
  \ technologies, through increasing understanding and creating the opportunities\
  \ for citizens to discuss the issues together.\nAs a part of this, we have worked\
  \ hard to ensure that Caf\xE9s are held across France, including outside of major\
  \ cities and in France's overseas territories (the DOM-TOMs). We work with a number\
  \ of different civil society groups to tailor Caf\xE9s to particular publics, notably\
  \ for young and elderly people, those with disabilities, and those from areas which\
  \ are often ignored in digital policy discussions.\nOur methods are focused on boosting\
  \ skills and involvement in dialogue - anyone can run a Caf\xE9 AI, without being\
  \ an expert, as long as they can ensure the discussion remains respectful, welcoming,\
  \ and that they do not express a particular position (eg promoting AI). Our resources,\
  \ designed with communities to foster debate, are freely available and shareable,\
  \ downloadable from our website.  We have also worked with those interested in other\
  \ countries to establish sister movements - the format is reproducible freely and\
  \ open to all. The movement is also frugal, centred on a small-group discussion\
  \ and simple activities - it is low-cost and easy to implement. Finally, the format\
  \ is extremely flexible, working equally well in a school, hospital, mountain-top,\
  \ or in the heart of a large business or government entity.\n\n\nFrom limitations:\
  \ Feel free to contact us for more information about Caf\xE9s not listed on the\
  \ site, including those in the past. "

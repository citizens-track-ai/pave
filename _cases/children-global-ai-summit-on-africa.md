# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "**5Rights and partners convened the first-ever\
  \ Children\u2019s Global AI Summit on Africa in Kigali, Rwanda bringing together\
  \ 50 children from across the continent to discuss the impact of artificial intelligence\
  \ on their lives.**\n\nHeld alongside the Global AI Summit on Africa, young participants\
  \ from  Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, and\
  \ South Sudan shared their perspectives on the potential and risks of AI. Through\
  \ interactive discussion, panel sessions and a learning session the children explored\
  \ how AI shapes their education, digital safety, and future job opportunities.\n\
  \nWhile they saw AI as a tool for learning and creativity, the children participating\
  \ also voiced concerns about its impact on their critical thinking and social skills,\
  \ privacy, and commercial exploitation and misinformation. They identified barriers\
  \ preventing children from meaningfully participating in AI governance, including\
  \ limited access to AI education, lack of awareness, and exclusion from policy discussions.\
  \ The children also shared solutions, calling on policymakers, governments, and\
  \ tech companies to prioritise AI education in schools, establish ethics and rights-respecting\
  \ AI regulations, and create platforms where children\u2019s voices can influence\
  \ AI decisions.\n\nThe Summit underscored the urgent need for AI to be designed\
  \ with children\u2019s rights in mind. General comment No. 25  and the African Committee\
  \ of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child are that clear children\u2019\
  s rights including safety and privacy, must be respected and protected across the\
  \ digital world.\n"

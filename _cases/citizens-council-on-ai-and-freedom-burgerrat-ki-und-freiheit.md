# Headline findings
headline-findings: '1. AI research should be communicated more transparently in clear
  language

  2. and suitable formats (websites, AI fairs, panel discussions, open house events,
  etc.).

  3. A central repository for proposals for AI research from citizens and public funding
  for the implementation of these proposals should be established.

  4. Measures should be implemented to inform citizens about the significant potential
  of donating their anonymized health data to AI research and empower them to make
  informed decisions.

  5. Permanent citizens'' councils should be convened to advise AI research on traffic-related
  issues such as autonomous driving, improved safety on cycle paths, the reduction
  of CO2 and particulate matter, and increased public transport capacity.

  6. Transparent communication regarding uncertainties surrounding AI applications,
  particularly in the transport and healthcare sectors, should be established to avoid
  creating a false sense of security.


  '

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "[From RHET AI website] The Citizens'\
  \ Council combines science communication with deliberative citizen participation\
  \ to constructively incorporate the diverse perspectives of citizens, alongside\
  \ scientific viewpoints, into debates and political decisions surrounding publicly\
  \ funded AI research. Because we are convinced: Together we know more. And the more\
  \ perspectives are included, the more informed and forward-looking the decisions\
  \ can be, serving the interests of society as a whole. \n\nIt is not a given that\
  \ all people can participate equally in such dialogue processes. The concept of\
  \ epistemic injustice describes how some individuals and groups participate in debates\
  \ on socially relevant topics under difficult conditions, as they are systematically\
  \ accorded less credibility and prevailing forms of expression and interpretation\
  \ are less tailored to their needs. \n\nAgainst this background, the citizens' council\
  \ was conceived as both a practical project and a scientific research field within\
  \ the framework of the RHET-AI collaboration between public engagement and science\
  \ communication research. \n"

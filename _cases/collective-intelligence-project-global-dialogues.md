# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "Collective Intelligence Project's Global\
  \ Dialogues on AI were launched at the 2024 AI Action Summit, and have taken place\
  \ regularly since, working with a range of sponsors and partners.\n\nIn the dialogues,\
  \ participants take part in an \"online collective-dialogue session\" of 15 - 60\
  \ minutes, hosted on the remesh.ai platform where they respond to structured prompts,\
  \ submit free text responses, and evaluate statements put forward by other participants.\
  \ This asynchronous process creates quantitative and qualitative measures of agreement\
  \ and difference.\n\nThe questions include longitudinal indicators, repeated regularly\
  \ in rounds of the dialogue to track change over time, and research questions to\
  \ test hypotheses posed by partners for each research round. These may use scenario\
  \ vignettes or open ended prompts to gather feedback.\n\nSessions might include\
  \ framing materials shared by a process moderator, and the remesh platform carries\
  \ out live analysis to surface statements that appear to have high consensus across\
  \ different demographics, so these get wider discussion. The analysis draws on bridging\
  \ algorithms. \n\nBy publishing an open dataset of Global Dialogue outcomes, the\
  \ project aims to inform a wide range of research and policy projects, and my including\
  \ partner-specific questions in specific dialogue rounds, it works to inform work\
  \ in particular policy areas, such as [a 2026 partnership with the Windfall Trust\
  \ \"exploring what the world truly needs, expects, hopes for, and fears from the\
  \ AI economy.\"](https://windfalltrust.org/global-dialogues-survey)\n \n"

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "In the context of rising concerns regarding\
  \ GenAI use, a **two-day deliberative process** was organized and hosted at the\
  \ University of Edinburgh\u2019s Edinburgh Futures Institute. A total of **17 students\
  \ from 6 disciplines**, gathered and deliberated on the question **\u201CHow can\
  \ we reimagine teaching, learning, and research methods to align with the advancements\
  \ in AI and ethics?\u201D**. In total, 6 deliberative sessions were facilitated\
  \ and 5 presentations were given by experts.\_\n\nAfter two days, the members of\
  \ the students\u2019 assembly presented 7 principles to guide the use of GenAI at\
  \ EFI. This output aimed to provide a strong and consensual signal that the University\
  \ of Edinburgh Futures Institute, as a future-oriented Institute of Higher Education\
  \ as well as a community of practice, must work towards a **bolder, more democratic,\
  \ fair, empathetic, sustainable and transparent approach to GenAI in teaching, learning\
  \ and research.**\n\nThe project took placed over a number of months, drawing upon\
  \ a pre-deliberative workshop to work with students to set the topic focus, and\
  \ interviews with experts to inform assembly design, followed by two days of deliberation,\
  \ and the presentation of a response, as well as inviting institutional responses\
  \ to the principles put forward by students. \n\n"

# What limitations to the project should be noted?
what-limitations-to-the-project-should-be-noted: "An [abstract submitted to the Participatory\
  \ AI Research & Practice Symposium](https://file.notion.so/f/f/b0312a1b-11ca-4b77-bf10-8cc57979536b/f68d7cf7-1ad0-454f-bf63-5944f2f0cc72/Extended_abstract_-_GenAI_in_HE_-_Deliberation.pdf?table=block&id=263260e2-4e1a-813d-8fa3-dc10f5572abc&spaceId=b0312a1b-11ca-4b77-bf10-8cc57979536b&expirationTimestamp=1782504000000&signature=asgkRRLMCwIlDYF42sqjXlrgliPk-RIMeAt61PA3kyk&downloadName=Extended+abstract+-+GenAI+in+HE+-+Deliberation.pdf)\
  \ explains that:\n> These findings are faced with several limitations, including\
  \ the small scale and limited duration of the students\u2019 assembly, which restricted\
  \ the diversity of expert perspectives and the ability to translate principles into\
  \ an actionable roadmap. Additionally, the ad hoc nature of the process, the lack\
  \ of institutionalization, and the absence of strong implementation mechanisms raise\
  \ concerns about the long-term relevance and impact of the outcomes, especially\
  \ given the evolving discussions around GenAI and broader challenges like the datafication\
  \ and neoliberalization of universities. Future research should explore whether\
  \ institutionalizing deliberative processes in higher education can ensure adaptability,\
  \ relevance, and effectiveness in addressing AI governance issues. The same research\
  \ could also be applied to governments\u2019 deliberative approach to AI governance.\n"

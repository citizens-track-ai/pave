provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "In 2025, an academic research team initiated\
  \ a process that used participatory arts, including multimedia documentary theatre\
  \ and a long table discussion, to involve approximately 20 people in an interactive\
  \ performance lecture to examine the interaction between humans and AI with the\
  \ intended outcomes of building participant capacity to engage with AI and creating\
  \ re-usable participation tools and methods.\n\nThis project brought together stakeholder\
  \ groups that usually don\u2019t share their attitudes to AI. This included voice\
  \ and movement artists, actors, leaders from the private and public sectors, students,\
  \ organisational consultants, technologists. It also, for the first time, introduced\
  \ artistic research and performance based approach to the British Computer Society\
  \ events series.\n"

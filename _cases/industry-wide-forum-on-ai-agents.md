# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "\"In November 2025, leading technology\
  \ companies\u2014Meta, Oracle, DoorDash, PayPal, Cohere, and Microsoft\u2014in partnership\
  \ with the Stanford Deliberative Democracy Lab organized a first-of-its-kind collaborative\
  \ effort to consult the public in shaping the future of AI agents: The Industry-Wide\
  \ Forum on AI Agents.\n\nThis Industry-Wide Forum brought together everyday people\
  \ to weigh in on tech policy and product development decisions where there are difficult\
  \ tradeoffs with no simple answers. Specifically, participants in the Industry-Wide\
  \ Forum explored the use of AI agents, a type of generative AI that can perform\
  \ actions for you, on your behalf autonomously. In the near term, AI agents are\
  \ expected to conduct a myriad of transactions and tasks on behalf of users, opening\
  \ up considerable opportunities to offer great value as well as significant risks.\
  \ \n\nConsidering everything from personal to financial, medical, work use cases,\
  \ and more, participants discussed not only what a world with AI agents would look\
  \ like, but also how that world would fit them. These deliberations highlight what\
  \ users are thinking now amidst the developing landscape of AI agents. \n\n503 participants\
  \ from two countries, the United States and India, participated in this deliberative\
  \ event. The samples of these countries were recruited independently, so the Industry-Wide\
  \ Forum should be seen as two independent deliberations. In addition, 519 persons\
  \ participated in the control group, where the participants did not deliberate in\
  \ any discussions; the control group only completed the two surveys, pre and post.\
  \ The main purpose of the control group is to demonstrate that any changes that\
  \ occur after deliberation are a result of the deliberative event.\"\n"

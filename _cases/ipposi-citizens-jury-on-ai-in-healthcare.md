# Headline findings
headline-findings: "1. Equitable access: The use of AI-enabled care should be equitably\
  \ accessible to all, and there should be no distinction between care accessed publicly\
  \ or privately.\n2. Patient rights: The implementation of the EU AI Act should be\
  \ enshrined in national legislation, and there should be special provision to protect\
  \ health data relating to AI.\n3. National strategy: A national strategy on the\
  \ use of AI in healthcare in Ireland should be published in 2026.\n4. Multi-year\
  \ investment: The Government\u2019s budget should allocate adequate, multiannual\
  \ funding to begin investment in quality AI systems for healthcare in Ireland.\n\
  5. Regulatory body: A fully-representative, multi-disciplinary statutory body should\
  \ be established in 2025 to regulate and enforce standards around the use of AI\
  \ in healthcare, including licensing, compliance monitoring, and imposing penalties.\n\
  6. Digitally-enabled care: The Digital for Care Framework, in particular the solution\
  \ for a national electronic health record system, should be urgently and fully implemented\
  \ to provide the foundations for the deployment of quality AI solutions.\n7. Informed\
  \ consent (AI training): The health data used to train AI systems should be transparent\
  \ and inclusive, and the auto-enrolment of everyone\u2019s health data into the\
  \ training of AI should include an option to \u201Copt out\u201D.\n"

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "A citizens\u2019 jury is a deliberative\
  \ process whereby members of the public explore a complex issue (or \u2018tricky\u2019\
  \ topic) and develop recommendations to address and overcome key challenges and\
  \ concerns.\_IPPOSI Citizens\u2019 Juries are guided by an independent oversight\
  \ panel comprising of experts in the field of healthcare delivery, health policy,\
  \ health information management, as well as patient advocacy and public engagement.\
  \ The 2024 IPPOSI Citizens\u2019 Jury was designed to include three phases, offering\
  \ individuals the opportunity to engage in meaningful dialogue [inquiry, deliberation,\
  \ verdict], in a semistructured way and to produce an output that is representative\
  \ of their concerns, preferences and expectations. The jurors were posed the question,\
  \ SHOULD WE INCREASE THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) IN HEALTHCARE IN IRELAND?\n"

# Headline findings
headline-findings: "1. Students were asked if they used GenAI when they weren't allowed\
  \ - the message is clear - the majority of students are honest the majority of the\
  \ time.\n2. There is a discourse in the media that all students are using GenAI\
  \ - this is not the case with our data - 70% were users and 30% with non users.\n\
  3. The main motivations for using GenAI are efficiency,\n4. The drive for efficiency\
  \ is balanced by the desire for students to act ethically - students want to do\
  \ the work themselves, they worry about the ethical and enivronmental impact of\
  \ GenAI.\n5. Students are using GenAI for a number of tasks related to the learning\
  \ and assessment process but are stopping short, in most cases, of directly editing\
  \ their assessments. The problem is, we are unsure what impact the cognitive offloading\
  \ of reading, brainstorming and structuring of assessments has on students. \n"

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "This project consisted of a survey of\
  \ over 7000 UK university students, interviews with 20 students and a GenAI assisted\
  \ scoping review. This research project adopted a mixed-methods approach, integrating\
  \ both qualitative and quantitative research methods, to comprehensively explore\
  \ students' experiences and attitudes towards the use of AI tools in UK higher education.\
  \ \n\nIt had 4 key aims. 1.) To examine UK university students' perceived usefulness\
  \ and acceptance of AI tools for learning. 2.) To investigate the diverse uses of\
  \ AI tools in academic tasks, their frequency, and the underlying reasons for these\
  \ usage patterns among UK university students. 3.) To explore both the challenges\
  \ and facilitators affecting the adoption and wider utilisation of AI tools in UK\
  \ universities, including both user-level and system-level factors. 4). To co-develop\
  \ evidence-based recommendations for enhancing AI tools use and implementation in\
  \ the education sector, in collaboration with relevant communities such as students,\
  \ educators, university administrators, and AI developers.\n"

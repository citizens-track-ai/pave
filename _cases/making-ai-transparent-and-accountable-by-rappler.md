# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "In 2023, OpenAI commissioned Rappler\
  \ to develop a process that used surveys and focus groups to involve approximately\
  \ 4,121 people to develop a platform for participatory governance on AI. \n\nRappler\u2019\
  s project aimed to ensure the following:\n- A process that includes and considers\
  \ the experiences of the Global South in generating global policies on AI.\_\n-\
  \ A process that\u2019s inclusive, representing diverse viewpoints and grassroots\
  \ concerns within the global context.\_\n\nStep 1\n\n**Participant selection and\
  \ clustering**\n\nSelect participants using online surveys conducted via Rappler's\
  \ website, external platforms, and partner organizations. Learn about the demographics\
  \ of participants and gauge their approach to reasoning about complex topics. Use\
  \ survey data to form clusters of respondents for the focus group discussions.\n\
  \nStep 2\n\n**Offline focus group discussions**\n\nConduct in-person focus group\
  \ discussions with a smaller number of participants (e.g., ~100). These discussions\
  \ are human-moderated and done in small, private groups of participants with shared\
  \ demographics. Participants discuss questions and prompts at a higher level, and\
  \ idea synthesis and initial policy ideas are generated using an LLM based on transcripts\
  \ of the discussions that are AI-generated and human-reviewed.\n\nStep 3\n\n**AI\
  \ moderator development**\n\nDevelop an AI moderator that is informed by a variety\
  \ of data sources for the online focus group discussions.\n\nStep 4\n\n**Online\
  \ focus group discussions**\n\nConduct online focus group discussions with a larger\
  \ number of participants (e.g., ~500). These discussions are AI-moderated and done\
  \ in groups of 3-100 participants. Participants answer questions and prompts in\
  \ parallel and their answers are summarized and presented to the public for voting\
  \ and commenting. Results are made viewable to the general public, and Rappler creates\
  \ stories explaining the nuances of the results.\n\nStep 5\n\n**Alignment compared**\n\
  \nCompare the results from the online and offline focus group discussions to understand\
  \ similarities and differences, and to determine next steps in terms of policy ideas.\n"

# Headline findings
headline-findings: 'Accuracy: Midjourney manages to convey binary gender presentations
  (i.e., feminine women and masculine men). Regarding nonbinary genders, the AI model
  may be able to convey a broad idea of what nonbinarity may be, but if fails with
  specific nuances. However, even our queer participants did not have a  clear shared
  idea of how specific nonbinary genders appear. Therefore, accuracy in specific nonbinary
  genders is a sociotechnical chimaera.


  Discrimination: Participants denounced Midjourney for having racist, patriarchal,
  fatphobic, ageist, reproductivist, sexist, pornographic, and cisnormative assumptions.
  Importantly, these assumptions are differently distributed across the Gender Diamond
  interactive visualisation of the gender spectrum. For instance, participants stated
  that agender people were usually portrayed as older, poorer, and fat. Differently,
  demiwomen were usually infantilised and sexualised.


  Speculation: Three different queer scenarios emerged for the generation of AI-generated
  gendered portraits, by differently prioritising accuracy, nondiscrimination, or
  critical engagement.

  '

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "In 2025, the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano\
  \ and Eurac Research initiated a process that involved approximately 60 people in\
  \ participatory workshops designed to explore approaches to audit gender bias in\
  \ AI-generated portraits with the intended outcomes of reviewing AI design implementations\
  \ and creating re-usable participation tools.\n\nThe project explicitly sought to\
  \ work with queer people and their allies across three sites in Italy, Austria,\
  \ and Finland and invited participants to engage with AI-generated gendered portraits\
  \ through a critical design interface, which is still available on the project website.\
  \ \n\nThe interface guided discussions in terms of accuracy (i.e., how well the\
  \ portrait aligns with the participant's gender stereotypes), discrimination (i.e.,\
  \ whether participants noticed problematic associations), and speculation  (i.e.,\
  \ how participants imagined alternative queer human\u2013AI interactions for AI-generated\
  \ gendered portraits).\n"

# Why is this project important to include in the case book?
why-is-this-project-important-to-include-in-the: "\"NonbAInary\" proposes a nonbinary\
  \ way to engage with gender biases in generative AI. It systematically expands the\
  \ analysis by including nonbinary genders, such as \"masculine women\" or \"agender\
  \ people\", included in the Gender Diamond interactive datafication of the gender\
  \ spectrum. Indeed, nonbinary genders are usually dismissed by datafication practices,\
  \ thus being excluded from AI training datasets as well as AI audits: a double-binded\
  \ invisibilisation.\nFurthermore, \"NonbAInary\", being a series of participatory\
  \ workshops, included the voices of queer people and their allies, eliciting their\
  \ gender stereotypes, their perceptions of discrimination, and possible alternative\
  \ human\u2013AI scenarios."

# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "The Odyssean Process is a methodology\
  \ developed in 2023 designed to integrate expert elicitation with exploratory modelling\
  \ and scenario planning, culminating in a citizens\u2019 assembly. The framework\
  \ aims to address simultaneous systems failures across media, science, the economy,\
  \ and politics\u2014particularly regarding the management of systemic risks and\
  \ the integration of science with public reason in the face of accumulating tipping\
  \ points in Earth and social systems.\n\nThe inaugural full application of this\
  \ process is being delivered in Britain to assist with the global, long-term governance\
  \ of AI.\n- **May 2026:** Conclusion of the initial expert phases.\n- **June 2026:**\
  \ Release of interim findings and initial modelling. These outputs will support\
  \ a presentation in Geneva demonstrating how innovation and existing best practice\
  \ intersect for complex, long-term policy problems in AI.\n- **July 2026:** Commencement\
  \ of the Citizens' Assembly.\n- **September \u2013 November 2026:** Intense deliberative\
  \ phase of the assembly.\n- **December 2026 \u2013 Q2 2027:** Publication of a comprehensive\
  \ policy report, peer-reviewed papers, and a accompanying documentary.\n\n"

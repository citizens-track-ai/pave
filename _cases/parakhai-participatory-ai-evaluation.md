# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "Between 2024 and 2025, CivicDataLab initiated\
  \ a process of broad stakeholder engagement, involving technical, domain and cultural\
  \ (lived experience) experts to co-design an open source platform for participatory\
  \ AI evaluation. This case documents the design process to date. In future, use\
  \ of the platform should enable participatory audit of AI systems.  \n\n> CivicDataLab\
  \ has co-created ParakhAI - an open-source and participatory platform for AI evaluation.\
  \ The tool has been selected and supported by the Ministry of Electronics and Information\
  \ Technology and Digital India Programme\u2019s initiative for promoting fair, transparent\
  \ and ethical AI practices under the IndiaAI Mission\u2019s Safe and Trusted Pillar.\
  \ \n> \n> The tool is designed to identify bias, hallucination, informational integrity\
  \ issues in AI models to enable responsible and trustworthy AI deployment. It embeds\
  \ stakeholder participation from developers, technical, domain and cultural experts\
  \ across the AI lifecycle,moving beyond self-regulation. \n\nThe system consists\
  \ of two core components\n\n**A Web-based Evaluation tool for AI Models**: A user-friendly,\
  \ open-source tool offering an automated-assisted and expert evaluation environment\
  \ for AI models. This tool currently targets text-based models like reasoning, Q&A\
  \ and translation. It includes automated-assisted modules on Bias & Fairness, Misinformation\
  \ & Hallucination, and Safety & Privacy. It allows onboarding and registration of\
  \ independent experts, with technical, domain and cultural expertise. The final\
  \ output includes automated reports for evaluated parameters, compiling results\
  \ from evaluations into standardised, transparent formats, and providing actionable\
  \ insights for AI makers for continuous improvement.\n\n**Multilingual Prompt Databases**:\
  \ The tool will curate and integrate sectoral prompt database repositories across\
  \ Indian languages. They team encourage experts to contribute prompt datasets to\
  \ support ethical and responsive evaluation processes. \n"

# Why is this project important to include in the case book?
why-is-this-project-important-to-include-in-the: 'ParakhAI is significant for the
  case book because it moves AI evaluation beyond conventional self-regulation by
  developers, embedding structured participation from a diverse pool of stakeholders,
  technical experts, domain specialists, and cultural experts, directly into the evaluation
  lifecycle. This is particularly notable for a few reasons:


  Engaging underrepresented expertise in AI governance: The platform onboards independent
  experts with cultural and linguistic knowledge, not just technical AI expertise,
  ensuring evaluation reflects context-specific harms (bias, misinformation, safety
  issues) that purely technical audits often miss, especially across India''s linguistically
  diverse population.


  Multilingual and culturally grounded evaluation: The multilingual prompt databases,
  curated across Indian languages and contributed to by experts, represent a novel
  method for surfacing how AI models perform (or fail) outside English-dominant contexts,
  a perspective frequently absent from mainstream AI evaluation frameworks.

  Open, participatory, and transparent infrastructure: As an open-source tool built
  with government backing (IndiaAI Mission''s Safe and Trusted Pillar), it offers
  a replicable model for participatory AI auditing that other governments, civil society
  organizations, or AI developers could adopt, rather than a one-off evaluation exercise.

  Standardized, actionable outputs: By compiling diverse expert evaluations into standardized,
  transparent reports, the project demonstrates how participatory input can be translated
  into concrete, actionable feedback for AI developers, bridging the gap between public
  engagement and real technical improvement.'

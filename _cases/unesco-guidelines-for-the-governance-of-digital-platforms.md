# Provide a brief description of the project
provide-a-brief-description-of-the-project: "To expand the development of the _Guidelines\
  \ for the Governance of Digital Platforms_ beyond a narrow group of expert contributors,\
  \ UNESCO partnered with the Innovation for Policy Foundation (i4Policy) between\
  \ April and June 2023 to run an open grassroots consultation process, drawing on\
  \ distributed dialogue and deliberative workshop methods. \n\nMoving away from earlier\
  \ open public comment or conference formats, the engagement design used a three-stage\
  \ deliberative methodology\u2014learning, small-group deliberation, and anonymous\
  \ feedback. \n\ni4Policy facilitated a number of online and in-person deliberation\
  \ sessions, and provided resources, micro-grants and training to support local partners\
  \ or organise distributed deliberative consultation sessions. \n\nThese microgrants\
  \ directly removed financial barriers in under-resourced communities by covering\
  \ infrastructure costs like venue space and internet access. \n\nAs a result, the\
  \ initiative successfully gathered an additional 6,214 comments from 1,349 new participants\
  \ across 70 countries. This contributed to a substantial shift in the demographic\
  \ balance of the project, causing participation from the African continent to increase\
  \ from just 5% in the first phase to 54% in the third phase, and directly introduced\
  \  new policy concepts such as youth-led Media and Information Literacy (MIL) frameworks\
  \ into the final Guidelines. \n"

# What limitations to the project should be noted?
what-limitations-to-the-project-should-be-noted: "The primary insight derived from\
  \ this case study is that passive openness and digital accessibility alone are fundamentally\
  \ insufficient to ensure the representation of diverse global voices. While publishing\
  \ drafts online or hosting centralized international conferences creates a theoretical\
  \ channel for public engagement, these methods naturally default to sector-specific\
  \ professionals who already possess the resources and familiarity to participate.\
  \ Truly democratic and inclusive policy-making requires a proactive, resource-intensive\
  \ commitment to dismantle structural, financial, and technical barriers for marginalized\
  \ communities. As demonstrated by the i4Policy intervention\u2014where surveys revealed\
  \ that 72% of the decentralized participants had not engaged in any public consultation\
  \ in the previous six months\u2014meaningful inclusion hinges on a willingness to\
  \ invest both time and funding into grassroots partnerships. Furthermore, the process\
  \ highlighted that utilizing neutral local facilitators creates an essential environment\
  \ of trust, validating that early and authentic stakeholder engagement produces\
  \ a far more balanced, highly contextualized, and globally relevant framework. \n"

# Message
message: 7) We strive towards equality, inclusion and accessibility. GenAI must not
  be a tool that excludes individuals or creates further inequalities in EFI. Democratizing
  access to GenAI to support a variety of learning needs is crucial. GenAIs that are
  introduced in the learning experience should have user-friendly interfaces to accommodate
  accessibility needs, including those of students, staff or academics with disabilities.
  We aim to foster an inclusive and just environment where GenAI serves as a catalyst
  for learning.

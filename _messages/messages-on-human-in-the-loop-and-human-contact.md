# Name
name: 'Al should serve to help humans, not replace them


  We believe that people should not only have the last word at the end of a decision-making
  process in which Al is used but that they must be involved throughout the process
  to explain or intervene. This is clearly important when Al systems are used without
  our explicit consent and when there is high risk, for example, in medical diagnosis
  or loan decisions


  Transparency and trust are important and go hand in hand in this regard if people
  gain insight into how and why a decision was made, they will more readily accept
  the outcome


  This is all the more important for people less familiar with Al, or who do not have
  access to it, so that they are not excluded from society'

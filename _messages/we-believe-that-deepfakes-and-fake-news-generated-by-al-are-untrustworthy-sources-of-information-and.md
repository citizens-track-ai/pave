message: "We believe that deepfakes and fake news generated by Al are untrustworthy\
  \ sources of information and have a significant impact on our society they can influence\
  \ citizens, threaten the privacy of individuals (eg, by spreading fake nude images),\
  \ destroy reputations or create fake reputations, and easily spread false information.\
  \ This can encourage bullying. Therefore, we believe that deepfakes and fake news\
  \ should be considered high risk and not low risk as currently stated in the Al\
  \ Act. \n\nMoreover, just because there is legislation on illegal deepfake does\
  \ not mean that the problem is solved for people and democracyThis is an urgent\
  \ and important issue that should be at the top of the political and legal agenda\
  \ both nationally and at the European level\n\nWe also want a regulatory framework\
  \ to be implemented exclusively by EU political actors, so that the responsibilities\
  \ are not fragmented across a variety of actors"

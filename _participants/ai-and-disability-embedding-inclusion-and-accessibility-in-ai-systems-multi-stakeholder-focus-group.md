# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: 'Participants were purposively selected by the Nigerian AI
  Research Lab and Centre for Disability Inclusion Africa to ensure representation
  across seven disability clusters: visual impairment, hearing and speech impairment,
  mobility impairment, intellectual disabilities, dwarfism, and albinism. Selection
  also reflected diversity across age, educational background, and occupation. Specific
  inclusion efforts included recruiting across all seven disability clusters rather
  than focusing on the most visible or commonly represented groups, and including
  allied participants such as caregivers, parents, and technology developers to ensure
  that the discussion captured multiple perspectives on AI accessibility. The engagement
  was hosted at a university venue in Lagos accessible to participants with mobility
  needs.'

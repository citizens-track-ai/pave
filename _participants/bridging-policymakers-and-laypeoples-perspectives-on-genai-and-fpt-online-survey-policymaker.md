# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: "We aimed to target individuals involved in AI policymaking.\
  \ To do so, we defined two inclusion criteria: (1) the individual has been or is\
  \ actively involved in AI policymaking at a governmental institution; (2) the individual\
  \ has been or is actively advising AI policymakers. With AI policymaking, we refer\
  \ to the development of AI strategies or policies at any local, national, or international\
  \ level. We do not refer to AI strategies or policies introduced by industry players.\
  \ We contacted a total of 703 individuals from the European Parliament (N = 110),\
  \ the European Commission\u2019s High-Level Expert Group (N = 42), the Council of\
  \ Europe (N = 178), and national governments and their advisors (N = 373). To increase\
  \ the response rate, we included in the email invitation letter a request for the\
  \ contacted individuals to forward the survey invitations to relevant colleagues,\
  \ such as their staff, who often prepare recommendations or technical details for\
  \ AI policies or strategies. We collected data between 29 November 2023 and 12 December\
  \ 2023. After an initial invitation, we sent out two reminders to people who had\
  \ not completed the questionnaire at that time. The sample size is N = 61."

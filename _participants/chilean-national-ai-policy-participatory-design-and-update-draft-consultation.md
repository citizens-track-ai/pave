# Additional notes
additional-notes: The public consultation involved 209 respondents and was open online
  from 15 December 2020 to 27 January 2021. It invited individuals and organizations
  to review the draft National AI Policy, rate their agreement with its objective,
  principles and policy axes, and submit written comments. The consultation functioned
  as an accountability mechanism after the deliberative phase, allowing participants
  to assess and improve the draft. Respondents broadly supported the policy, with
  87.2% agreeing or strongly agreeing with the general objective and more than 80%
  agreeing or strongly agreeing with the proposed axes and objectives. Their comments
  led to changes in the final policy, including stronger emphasis on talent development,
  data protection, inclusive AI, territorial deliberation, labor impacts, cybersecurity,
  gender equity and responsible public-sector adoption.

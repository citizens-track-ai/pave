# Additional notes
additional-notes: "The final public consultation for the 2024 update of Chile\u2019\
  s National AI Policy was an online consultation focused on Axis 3: Governance and\
  \ Ethics. It was open from 15 January to 15 March 2024 and invited individuals,\
  \ legal entities and groups to submit written observations through a Google Form.\
  \ The process received input from 640 participants from all regions of Chile, including\
  \ 96.1% individuals, 3.6% legal entities and 0.3% non-profit organizations or groups.\
  \ It collected 5,760 agreement-level responses and around 2,214 complementary comments.\
  \ The consultation informed the final update by strengthening sections on regulation,\
  \ transparency, accountability, data protection, citizen participation, international\
  \ cooperation, climate and environmental impacts, gender equality, inclusion and\
  \ non-discrimination, labor impacts, children and adolescents, intellectual property,\
  \ culture and heritage, cybersecurity, disinformation and digital rights."

# Additional notes
additional-notes: "The RAM process included six thematic multi-stakeholder discussions\
  \ held in June and July 2023 across six Chilean regions, with more than 200 in-person\
  \ participants from academia, industry, the public sector and civil society, plus\
  \ parallel virtual expert discussions with more than 100 experts. The sessions focused\
  \ on the future of work, democracy, government, human-AI interaction in health/education/security,\
  \ regulation, and the environment. Using a backcasting methodology, participants\
  \ imagined possible AI futures for Chile toward 2050 and identified actions needed\
  \ today to achieve desirable scenarios and avoid harmful ones. These discussions\
  \ informed the RAM recommendations and supported the 2024 update of Chile\u2019\
  s National AI Policy, especially around ethics, regulation, governance, institutional\
  \ capacity, labor impacts, data protection, public-sector AI, education and environmental\
  \ sustainability."

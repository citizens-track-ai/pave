# Additional notes
additional-notes: 'The self-organized roundtables involved approximately 1,300 participants
  across 70 roundtables. These were organized by actors from academia, industry, civil
  society and other stakeholder networks after an open call from the Ministry. Groups
  could convene their own discussions and submit inputs structured around the three
  policy axes. The Ministry supported the process by offering guidance, a participation
  manual, optional presentations from public servants, and a sponsorship mechanism
  that gave official recognition to citizen- and stakeholder-led deliberation.


  The webinars played a capacity-building and outreach role. They helped create a
  shared baseline of understanding about AI by presenting concrete applications, risks
  and governance questions in different domains, from astronomy to privacy. This was
  especially important because the process aimed to move beyond expert-only discussion:
  the webinars made AI more accessible to broader publics, increased visibility of
  the policy process, and encouraged organizations and individuals to host or join
  self-organized roundtables. In total, 15 webinars reached more than 6,600 people.'

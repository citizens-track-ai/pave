recruitment-description: 'Participants were identified through secondary research
  and ecosystem mapping to locate organizations and individuals working on valuable
  multilingual language datasets. Outreach combined desk research with field visits,
  institutional meetings, workshops, webinars, conferences, and referrals from partners.
  Interested contributors entered the DOST onboarding process through Expressions
  of Interest (EOIs) or direct engagement. The project actively sought participation
  from organizations working with low-resource and underrepresented languages, ensuring
  contributors from government, academia, civil society, cultural institutions, and
  independent research were represented. '

# Additional notes
additional-notes: 'DOST adopts a facilitative rather than extractive model of engagement.
  Instead of collecting new datasets directly, the initiative works with organizations
  and communities that already working to create or are sitting on  valuable multilingual
  language resources, supporting them in identifying, preparing, documenting, and
  sharing datasets for AI use. Contributors remain owners of their datasets and determine
  licensing and access conditions, while receiving technical support, attribution,
  and long-term ecosystem engagement. The project seeks to establish sustainable data
  streams rather than one-time dataset contributions. '

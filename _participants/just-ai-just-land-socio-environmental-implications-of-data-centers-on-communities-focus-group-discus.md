# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: Participants were selected based on their direct or indirect
  engagement with the impacts of data center expansion. The recruitment approach aimed
  to include diverse social positions and lived experiences, including farmers affected
  by land acquisition, local governance representatives, frontline health workers,
  agricultural workers, and people from different caste and social groups. Specific
  efforts at inclusion included engaging marginalised communities and ensuring participation
  from groups often excluded from AI and digital infrastructure governance discussions.
# Was there a specific target population?
was-there-a-specific-target-population: The target population was local communities
  directly or indirectly affected by data center expansion, including farmers, women,
  ASHA (frontline health volunteers) workers, Panchayati Raj representatives, local
  residents, and other community stakeholders.

# Additional notes
additional-notes: In Begarikancha, focus group discussions included farmers, Panchayati
  Raj Institution representatives, and diverse community stakeholders. In Mekaguda,
  focus group discussions included farmers, ASHA workers, panchayat representatives,
  and other community members. The study followed ethical research practices including
  informed consent, confidentiality, voluntary participation, and sensitivity to local
  power dynamics. It is also an important consideration that 'community' is not a
  monolith category.

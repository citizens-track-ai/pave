# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: Participants were selected because of their direct lived
  experience of data center expansion or their specialised knowledge of the wider
  issues surrounding digital infrastructure. Local participants were identified through
  field-based engagement in affected or nearby communities, especially Mekaguda, Begarikancha,
  and surrounding villages. Expert participants were selected for their knowledge
  of land acquisition, water stress, power systems, labour, policy, digital infrastructure,
  journalism, and environmental governance.  Specific inclusion efforts included engaging
  people whose voices are often excluded from AI governance and digital infrastructure
  debates, including rural residents, farmers, women frontline workers, Anganwadi
  workers, ASHA workers, local community representatives, people affected by land
  acquisition, and members of communities experiencing livelihood and resource pressures.
  The interviews also included national and international expert perspectives to situate
  the local case within broader Indian and global debates on data centers and AI infrastructure.
# Was there a specific target population?
was-there-a-specific-target-population: The target population included community representatives,
  subject-matter experts, researchers, journalists and activists directly or indirectly
  engaged in advocating for community rights in the context of data center expansion,
  as well as people with relevant expertise on land governance, water resources, energy
  systems, labour, digital infrastructure, and environmental reporting.

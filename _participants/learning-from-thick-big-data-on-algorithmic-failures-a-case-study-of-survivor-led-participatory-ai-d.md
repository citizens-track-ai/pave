# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: 'VoiceValor began with hybrid interviews and listening sessions
  with 60 women, gender minorities, LGBTQI+ users, journalists, feminist creators,
  and survivors of technology-facilitated gender-based violence across South Asia.
  They described a pattern that many moderation systems still fail to see around online
  harm expressed through humor, culturally coded threats, caste and religious markers,
  sexualised humiliation, political abuse, local slang, and identity-based insinuation.
  Survivors kept flagging and reporting. We heard from their needs and ran a small
  beta platform to get their feedback. After integrating their feedback, we piloted
  the technology stack and an evaluation interface in a gathering of 700+ grassroots
  community leaders. Learning and co-creating with them helped us pilot and evaluate
  the web auditing in practice. The early feedback is very encouraging for the use
  of the methods and technology.  '

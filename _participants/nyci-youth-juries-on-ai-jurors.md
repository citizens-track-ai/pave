# Recruitment description
recruitment-description: "NYCI launched an open call for young  people to apply to\
  \ the citizen\u2019s juries.  Applicants were not asked to detail their  experience\
  \ or interest in AI. Instead, they  were required only to provide basic  contact\
  \ information and demographic  details through an online form. The form was openly\
  \ accessible to all  young people via NYCI\u2019s website. This  was complemented\
  \ by pro\u2011active  outreach on social media and emails to  more than 400 DEIS\
  \ schools, UBU Youth  Services, and Comhairle na n\xD3g groups. A code developed\
  \ by researchers at  the Insight SFI Research Centre for  Data Analytics in DCU\
  \ was then used  to randomly select 48 jurors across 6  juries while maintaining\
  \ the desired  demographic balance laid out in the  quotas. Naturally, not all selected\
  \ young  people would be able to participate. To  address this, 24 alternate jurors\
  \ were  also identified. Where a young person  withdrew from a jury, an alternate\
  \  juror was randomly selected to fill their  place."
# Was there a specific target population?
was-there-a-specific-target-population: "Using data available via the CSO  and other\
  \ sources, the project team  developed an estimated demographic  profile of Irish\
  \ youth. The selection  process aimed to ensure each of  the following demographics\
  \ were  represented on the juries.  \u2022 Young people of all gender identities.\
  \ \u2022 Young people of different ages. \u2022 Disabled young people  \u2022 LGBT+\
  \ young people \u2022 Young people from a variety of racial  and ethnic backgrounds\
  \ \u2022 Young people living in rural Ireland \u2022 Young people living in different\
  \  parts of Ireland and within areas  experiencing economic disadvantage. \u2022\
  \ Young people who left education  early. The estimated demographic  profile informed\
  \ the development of quotas and targets for the recruitment process"

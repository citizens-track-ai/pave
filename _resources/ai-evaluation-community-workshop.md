# Short description
short-description: "Building safe and trustworthy responsible AI systems requires\
  \ robust, participatory evaluation that moves from broad principles to applied and\
  \ localised context-sensitive systems rooted in Global South realities and aligned\
  \ with emerging global best practices. CivicDataLab (CDL) and Karya convened a roundtable\
  \ discussion  \u201CParticipatory AI Evaluation for Safe and Trusted  Systems \u201D\
  \ bringing together policymakers, international organisations, AI safety researchers,\
  \ technologists, and civil society practitioners to examine the role of participatory\
  \ evaluation of AI to enable a responsible and safe AI ecosystem to mitigate harms,\
  \ risks, & exclusion. CDL convened this dialogue to foster cross-sector collaboration,\
  \ share practical use cases, and identify pathways for responsible and inclusive\
  \ deployment of AI in public systems."

# Short description
short-description: This article presents findings from the Worker-Owned Intersectional
  Platforms (WOIP) action research project conducted between 2024 and 2025 with six
  worker-led organisations from Brazil and Argentina, including the MTST Technology
  Sector. The research examines how grassroots organisations, cooperatives, and social
  movements develop alternative technological futures through principles of care,
  intersectionality, democratic participation, and popular digital sovereignty.  The
  article documents the MTST Technology Sector's work on technological education in
  urban peripheries, the development of the "Hire Those Who Struggle" (Contrate Quem
  Luta) platform, and its broader vision of Popular Digital Sovereignty as a framework
  for democratic participation in technology governance.

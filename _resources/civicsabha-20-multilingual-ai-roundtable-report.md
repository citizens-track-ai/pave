# Short description
short-description: his report documents the discussions and recommendations from the
  Multilingual and Inclusive Datasets for Accessible AI roundtable held during CivicSabha
  2.0, an official pre-summit event of the India AI Impact Summit 2026. Bringing together
  government, academia, civil society, AI developers, researchers, and language communities,
  the report explores participatory approaches to multilingual AI, language data stewardship,
  Digital Public Infrastructure, community-centred dataset creation, and inclusive
  AI governance. The recommendations and discussion themes directly informed the DOST
  initiative's approach to building sustainable multilingual data ecosystems.

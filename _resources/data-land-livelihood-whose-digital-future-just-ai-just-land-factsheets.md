# Short description
short-description: 'Data, Land & Livelihood: Whose Digital Future? is a factsheet
  series developed under the Just AI, Just Land project. It presents the socio-environmental
  implications of data-centre expansion in Telangana through concise, visually accessible
  factsheets. The resource highlights how AI infrastructure depends on land, water,
  energy, and public systems, and asks critical questions about who benefits from
  digital growth, who bears its environmental and livelihood costs, and how communities
  can have a meaningful voice in shaping a just digital future.'

# Short description
short-description: The Language Data Collaborative is an open platform developed by
  CivicDataLab to improve the discoverability, accessibility, and responsible reuse
  of multilingual language datasets across India. The platform brings together datasets,
  contributors, organizations, AI use cases, and supporting resources to foster collaboration
  among government, academia, civil society, researchers, and language communities.
  Beyond dataset discovery, it documents real-world AI use cases to enable cross-learning
  across sectors, languages, and countries, helping practitioners understand how different
  datasets have been applied, what has worked in practice, and the uptake and impact
  of language datasets in diverse AI applications. As an extension of the DOST initiative,
  it promotes participatory stewardship of multilingual language resources and strengthens
  Digital Public Infrastructure for inclusive AI.
